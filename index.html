What it is

Discover a plant, save it, grow it.

Rooted is an iOS companion for serious home gardeners. Photograph any plant for instant identification and health analysis. Track every watering, journal entry, and harvest in one place — built for people who care about doing it well.

Three things it does

Built for serious gardeners, not casual houseplant owners.

Diagnose

Diagnose by photograph.

Point your camera at any plant or leaf. Get instant species identification, health diagnosis, and specific treatment guidance — powered by AI trained on serious gardening, not casual houseplant care.

Journal

A long memory for your garden.

Every watering, harvest, journal entry, and photograph in one place. Look back at last season to plan this one. Watch the rhythm of your garden emerge across years.

Streak

A streak that rewards growing, not scrolling.

Opening the app doesn't count. A care action does — a watering, a journal entry, a harvest. Show up for your plants, and Rooted shows up for you.
