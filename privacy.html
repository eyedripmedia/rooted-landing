This policy applies only to the Site. The Rooted iOS application, when released, will have its own separate Privacy Policy reflecting the additional information that application collects. This policy will be updated, or a separate policy published, at that time.

Information we collect

If you submit your email address through the waitlist form on the Site, we collect:

  • Your email address

We do not collect names, locations, payment information, or any other identifying information through the Site.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or social media pixels on the Site.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) and DNS provider (Cloudflare) may log standard technical information such as IP addresses and user agents for security and operational purposes. We do not access these logs for marketing purposes.

How we use your information

We use the email address you provide solely to:

  • Notify you when Rooted launches
  • Send occasional updates about the product's development, with your continued consent

We do not sell your email address or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Email service providers

When we begin sending email updates, we may use a third-party email service provider (such as Beehiiv) to manage the mailing list. That provider will process your email address on our behalf, under their own privacy and security commitments. This Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the specific provider before any such transfer occurs.

Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Unsubscribe. Every email we send (when we begin sending emails) will include an unsubscribe link.
  • Request deletion. Email privacy@growwithrooted.com to request that we permanently delete your email address from our records.
  • Request access. Email privacy@growwithrooted.com to ask what information we hold about you.

Texas residents have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act ("TDPSA"), including the right to access, correct, or delete their personal information and the right to opt out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@growwithrooted.com.

California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other state residents may have similar rights under their respective privacy laws.

Retention

We retain your email address until you ask us to delete it, or until twelve months after the application launches if you have not engaged with our communications, whichever comes first.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. Email submissions are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information to the Site, please contact privacy@growwithrooted.com and we will delete it promptly.

International users

The Site is intended for use by residents of the United States. We do not currently offer services in the European Union, United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions with comprehensive privacy regulations beyond U.S. state privacy laws.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this Privacy Policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If you have provided your email address, we will notify you of material changes by email.
